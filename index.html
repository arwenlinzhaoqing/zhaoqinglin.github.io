About me

Hi, I'm Zhao-Qing Lin, and my Chinese name is 林 兆青. You can call me Qing, which means cyan in Chinese.

I am a physics student with a passion of asking the “why” behind things. Trained in theoretical physics and experienced in hands-on instrumentation, I am interested in fundamental physics, precision measurement, gravity, Beyond the Standard Model physics, atomic physics, and quantum field theory.

Outside research, I enjoy art, culture, movement, and all kinds of little adventures. Welcome to my personal space, where I share my research, thoughts, and moments from a curious life.
